A hollow pleated part with 9 pleats, wider at the top. Bottom disc is 44 mm diameter, 10 mm thick (z 0..10). Top disc is 96 mm diameter, 10 mm thick (z 70..80). Between them (z 10..70) is a 9-point star frustum. At z=10, tip radius is 20 mm; at z=70, tip radius is 46 mm. Groove bottoms are at 50% of the tip radius. The first tip is rotated 10 degrees from the +X axis. The part is hollowed with a uniform 4 mm wall thickness. A blind bore of 16 mm diameter and 8 mm depth is cut into the bottom face on the axis.
